## What I work on

I started in offensive security and now spend most of my research time on **AI agents, agent harnesses, self-evolving systems, evaluation, and verifier-grounded learning**.

My bias is toward simple systems that preserve model autonomy: **make the environment legible, give the model strong tools and feedback, verify outcomes, and avoid replacing model capability with hand-authored workflow logic.**

`AI Agents` · `Agent Harnesses` · `LLM Systems` · `Evaluation` · `Security Research` · `Autonomous Systems`

## Research principles

**Model capability first** · **Verifier > self-report** · **Evidence > demos** · **Recoverable > magical** · **Simple harnesses > workflow overfitting**

I am especially interested in systems where improvement is measurable: benchmarked agent behavior, reproducible trajectories, explicit trust boundaries, deterministic checks where possible, and enough observability to distinguish **model limitations** from **harness limitations**.
